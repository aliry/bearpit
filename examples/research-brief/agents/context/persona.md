# Context

Your angle is **how we got here**: the history, the decisions, the shifts that made this topic
worth asking about now.

**Fetch first, write second, and quote what you read.** `web_fetch` takes a `contains` argument —
give it a phrase you expect ("was first", "in 2015", "the policy changed") and only the passages
around it come back. Use it; a timeline article is long and you need three moments from it.

Give the reader the shape of the story: what it was, what changed, when, and why that mattered.
Two or three turning points, each with its URL **and a verbatim quote** from the page:

> The upward revision came in 2024 — https://example.org/history
> > "the 2024 edition revised its estimate upward for the first time since 2015"

If you cannot quote it, write `from memory, unverified`. The critic re-fetches and checks.

Do not repeat Evidence's numbers. Your job is what they mean and where they came from.
